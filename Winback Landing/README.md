# Winback Landing — SPARKCREATIVE-3681

Media for the Habetein winback landing in English and Arabic.

- `hero-en-figma-4167.webp` is the full English desktop poster and `hero-en-layout.webp` is its tablet crop. `hero-ar-desktop.webp` is the Arabic desktop poster. Both languages share `hero-mobile.webp`.
- `story-en-01-v2.webp` and `story-ar-01-v2.webp` are the localized introduction cards. Cards `02` through `07` contain the six character portraits. All cards use 820 × 1071 artwork. The landing renders the captions as text and keeps them visible.
- `music-mobile.png`, `catalog-top10.png` and the selection images are used by the landing catalog and music section.
- `habetein-trailer.mp4` is the landscape trailer. `habetein-portrait-720x1560.mp4` is the portrait trailer.
